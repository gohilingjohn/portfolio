The workflow

One pipeline, end to end

Every project runs through the same loop — simple on purpose, boring on purpose. Boring means it keeps running.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Ideas and briefs land in one place — a folder, a chat, a prompt. Nothing gets lost, nothing needs chasing.

  2. 02

    Build

    AI and automation produce the assets: images, captions, videos, quote-cards — generated, checked, approved.

  3. 03

    Orchestrate

    Bots queue the work, schedule it and dispatch it across platforms — from one control panel.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Content goes live on schedule. Twice a day, every day — without anyone watching the clock.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Watchdogs and stats watch everything, flag what breaks, and feed what works back into the loop.
